Sources, Corrections & Privacy

Official records are linked whenever available. Commentary and petitions are identified separately from government documents. We do not treat an allegation as a proven fact.

If you identify an error, send the relevant document and a correction request through our community Facebook group. Verified corrections should be clearly noted alongside the original report.

Bill watchlists are stored locally in your browser, not on our servers. External links, including petitions and Facebook, have their own privacy practices.

What to Say

State your name and district, identify the bill number, say whether you support or oppose it, explain why, and politely request a response.

Tip: Contact your own district legislators for constituent assistance. Confirm hearing procedures and deadlines with the relevant committee. Messages to the Governor's Office may be subject to public disclosure.
